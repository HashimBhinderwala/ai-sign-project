Emergency Mode

Instantly Signal for Help

Emergency Mode is a critical feature for urgent situations. It allows you to use sign language to immediately alert authorities, connecting you to help when you can't speak or hear.

Activate Emergency Mode

For Urgent Use Only

This feature is designed to connect you directly with local emergency services. The app will translate your signed message and automatically share your current location to ensure a fast and accurate response. Only use this in genuine emergencies.

How it Works

  1. Press the "Activate Emergency Mode" button.
  2. Sign your message clearly to the camera (e.g., "Police," "Fire," "Medical Help").
  3. The app translates the sign and sends your request and location to authorities.

When to Use Emergency Mode

  • Medical emergencies like an injury or sudden illness.
  • Reporting a fire, car accident, or other immediate dangers.
  • Requesting police assistance during a threat or crime.
  • Any situation where you are unable to speak or hear to call for help.
