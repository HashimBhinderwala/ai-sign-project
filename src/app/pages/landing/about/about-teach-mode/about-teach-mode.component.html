Teach Mode

Personalize Gesture Learning

Teach Mode is a new interactive feature that lets users teach the app custom words and the corresponding signs. Personalize recognition, expand vocabulary, and share your taught sign–word pairs.

Learn More

Abstract

Teach Mode enables users to extend the app’s vocabulary by pairing custom words with user-provided sign samples. The app stores compact representations of signs for future recognition, supporting personal, regional, and domain-specific gestures.

How it works

  1. Record or upload a sign clip.
  2. Associate a word or phrase with it.
  3. Save — the model stores a descriptor for recognition.

Example Use Cases

  • Local slang or regional signs
  • Domain-specific gestures
  • Personal shorthand with friends/family
